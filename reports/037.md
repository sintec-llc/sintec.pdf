## Suggested next steps (for whoever syncs)
1. Syncing tag by tag is impractical: v0.12.0, v0.14.0, v0.20.0 and v0.21.0 are 700 to 1060 commits each. A better plan is to first rebuild our changes on the v0.15.0 module layout and egui 0.37, and settle the i18n question (craft-i18n with Russian, or our own catalogue). Then take the rest: security, then fonts and text, then features.
2. Do not cherry-pick the 60 open `fix/sec-*` branches; wait until upstream merges them. Re-check their state with `git merge-tree --write-tree upstream/main <branch>` (an unchanged tree means the change is already in main).
3. Keep the clean-room and asset rules (AGENTS.md §1) in force for everything pulled in. Fonts stay out of the repo, and new assets need `ATTRIBUTION.toml` entries.
4. New external build inputs: `CRAFT_I18N_DIR` and the storytold/egui and storytold/winit forks. Our CI and release jobs will need them.
